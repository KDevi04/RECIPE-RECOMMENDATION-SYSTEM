Szechuan Tofu & Green Bean Stir-Fry

Duration: 40 minutes

Ingredients

¼ cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
4 cups green beans, trimmed and cut in half
2 teaspoons Chinkiang vinegar
1/4teaspoon crushed red pepper
1 teaspoon plus 2 tablespoons cornstarch, divided
2 tablespoons canola oil, divided
1/2 tbs SALT & PEPPER
4 CLOVES GARLIC
2 tbs OF GINGER
1 1/4-Pounce package extra-firm tofu, drained


Directions:

Step 1: Whisk 1/4 cup water, soy sauce, tomato paste, vinegar, sugar, crushed red pepper to taste and 1 teaspoon cornstarch in a small bowl. Set aside.
Step 2: Cut tofu into 1/2- to 3/4-inch cubes and pat dry. Toss the tofu in a bowl with the remaining 2 tablespoons cornstarch to coat.
Step 3:Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the tofu and spread out across the surface of the pan.
Step 4: Let cook undisturbed for 2 minutes. Gently turn and stir.
Step 5: Continue cooking, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and crispy, 2 to 3 minutes more. Transfer to a plate.
Step 6: Reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil to the pan.Add green beans, garlic and ginger; cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute.
Step 7: Add the remaining 1/4 cup water, cover and cook until the beans are crisp-tender, 2 to 4 minutes.Stir the reserved soy sauce mixture and pour it over the green beans.
Step 8: Cook, stirring, until thickened, about 1 minute. Add the tofu and cook, stirring, until heated through, about 1 minute more.
