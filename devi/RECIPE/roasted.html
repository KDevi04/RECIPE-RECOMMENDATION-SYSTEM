White Bean Soup

Duration: 40 minutes

Ingredients

1 ONION & 2 CARROTS
1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
1/4 CUP OLIVE OIL
4 cups reduced-sodium vegetable broth
2 cups frozen chopped collard greens, thawed
1/2 tbs SALT & PEPPER
6 CLOVES GARLIC


Directions:

Step 1: Combine broth, sweet potatoes, collard greens, beans, onion, celery, lemon juice, garlic, garlic-and-herb seasoning, salt, pepper and crushed red pepper in a large Dutch oven. Bring to a boil over high heat
Step 2: Cover, reduce heat to medium and cook, undisturbed, until the sweet potatoes are tender, about 10 minutes.
Step 3:Stir in cream. Bring to a simmer, uncovered, over medium heat; simmer for 5 minutes. Divide among 6 bowls. Top with Parmesan.
