BEET SALAD

Duration: 40 minutes

Ingredients

5-6 beetroots
2tbs OF SHERRY VINEGAR
1/4 CUP OLIVE OIL
1/2tbs DIJON MUSTARD
1/2tbs FRESH HONEY
1/2 tbs SALT & PEPPER


Directions:

Step 1: Preheat the oven to 400˚F (200˚C). Divide the beets between two pieces of foil, then bring the edges together and crimp to make packets.
Step 2: Place the packets on a baking sheet and roast until the beets are just tender when pierced with the point of a knife, about 1 1/4 hours. Unwrap the beets and let them cool.
Step 3: While the beets are cooling, whisk together olive oil, vinegar, mustard, honey, salt, and pepper in a small bowl to make the dressing.
Step 4: Once the beets are cool enough to handle, slip off the skins, cut them into 1/2-inch cubes, and place them in a large bowl.
Step 5: Add celery and shallot to the bowl with the beets.
Step 6: Pour the dressing over the beet mixture and toss to coat well.
Step 7: Serve the salad at room temperature or chilled.
