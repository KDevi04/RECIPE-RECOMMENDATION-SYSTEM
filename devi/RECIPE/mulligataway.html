Vegetable Mulligatawny Soup

Duration: 40 minutes

Ingredients

1 ONION , 2 CARROTS , 1 PARSNIP FINELY CHOPPED
4 cups peeled diced acorn squash or butternut squash
3 tbs OLIVE OIL
4 cups reduced-sodium vegetable broth
1 medium green apple, peeled and finely chopped
1 can no-salt-added diced tomatoes
1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
3 cloves garlic, minced, divided
2 whole-wheat naan flatbreads, halved


Directions:

Step 1: Preheat oven to 375°F. Line a baking sheet with foil.Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large saucepan over medium heat until shimmering. Add onion, carrots and parsnip and cook until the onions are translucent, about 6 minutes.
Step 2: Add squash, apple, curry powder, 2 cloves garlic and ginger and cook, stirring, until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes. Add broth, tomatoes and lentils and stir to combine.
Step 3: Bring to a boil. Reduce heat to maintain a low simmer, cover and cook until the squash and lentils are tender, about 20 minutes.
Step 4: Meanwhile, brush one side of each naan with the remaining 1 tablespoon oil. Sprinkle with the remaining 1 clove garlic and place on the prepared baking sheet.
Step 5: Bake until warmed, 5 to 6 minutes. Remove from oven and sprinkle with cilantro.Gently mash some of the soup with a potato masher to achieve desired consistency.Garnish the soup with cilantro and serve with the naan.
