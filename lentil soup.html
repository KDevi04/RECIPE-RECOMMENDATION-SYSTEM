VEG LENTIL SOUP

Duration: 30 minutes

Ingredients

DRIED LENTILS 1 CUP
ONION 1,CHOPPED
CARROT 2,DICED
CELERY 2 STALKS,CHOPPED
VEGETABLE BROTH 4 CUPS
TOMATO 2 DICED
Garlic 2 CLOVES
DRIED THYME 1 TABLESPOON
SALT & PEPPER AS PER TASTE


Directions:

Step 1: In a large pot, combine lentils, chopped onion, carrots, celery, vegetable broth, diced tomatoes, garlic, and thyme.
Step 2: Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 25-30 minutes, until lentils and vegetables are tender..
Step 3: Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot with crusty bread or crackers.
