Shrimp Stir-Fry

Duration: 20-25 minutes

Ingredients

shrimp 150 grams
mixed vegetables(optional)
soya sauce 1 tablespoon
garlic and ginger 1 teaspoon
corn starch 1/2 teaspoon


Directions:

Step 1: Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels.Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
Step 2: Heat vegetable oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add minced garlic and ginger, stir-fry for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
Step 3: Add shrimp and cook until they turn pink, usually 2-3 minutes Add the mixed vegetables to the wok and stir-fry for an additional 3-4 minutes until they are slightly tender but still crisp.
Step 4: Pour soy sauce and sesame oil over the shrimp and vegetables. Toss everything together until well coated.
Step 5: If you prefer a thicker sauce, mix cornstarch with a little water to create a slurry. Add it to the stir-fry and stir until the sauce thickens.
Step 6: Serve the shrimp and vegetable stir-fry over a bed of cooked brown rice or quinoa.Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped green onions if desired.
