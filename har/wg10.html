Pork and Sweet Potato Hash

Duration: 40 minutes

Ingredients

Pork loin (200g, diced)
Sweet potatoes (1 cup, diced)
Onion (1, chopped)
Olive oil (1 tbsp)
Paprika (1 tsp)


Directions:

Step 1: Cook diced pork in a pan with olive oil.
Step 2: Add chopped onions and diced sweet potatoes.
Step 3: Season with paprika and cook until tender.
Step 4: Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.
Step 5: Serve hot.
