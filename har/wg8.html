Egg and Cheese Breakfast Burrito

Duration: 20 minutes

Ingredients

Eggs (2, scrambled)
Flour tortilla (1 large)
Cheddar cheese (1/2 cup, shredded)
Sausage links (2, cooked and sliced)
Salsa (2 tbsp)


Directions:

Step 1: Scramble eggs in a pan.
Step 2: Warm the tortilla and place it on a plate.
Step 3: Layer with scrambled eggs, sausage slices, and cheese.
Step 4: Add salsa to taste.
Step 5: Roll up the burrito and enjoy.
