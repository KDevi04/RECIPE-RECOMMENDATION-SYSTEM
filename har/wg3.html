BBQ Chicken Pizza

Duration: 35 minutes

Ingredients

pizza dough 850g
Cooked Chicken Breast 200g
BBQ Sauce (1/2 cup)
Red Onion (1/2, thinly sliced)
Mozzarella Cheese (1 cup)


Directions:

Step 1: Roll out pizza dough and spread BBQ sauce.
Step 2: Add shredded chicken, sliced red onion, and mozzarella.
Step 3: Bake according to pizza dough instructions.
Step 4: Slice and enjoy.
