Salmon and Quinoa Bowl

Duration: 30 minutes

Ingredients

Salmon Fillet (200g)
Quinoa (1 cup, cooked)
Avocado (1/2, sliced)
calories Cherry Tomatoes (1 cup)
Olive Oil (1 tbsp)


Directions:

Step 1: Grill or bake the salmon until cooked through.
Step 2: Cook quinoa according to package instructions.
Step 3: Assemble the bowl with quinoa, salmon, avocado, and cherry tomatoes.
Step 4: Drizzle olive oil over the top.
Step 5: Enjoy a nutritious meal!
