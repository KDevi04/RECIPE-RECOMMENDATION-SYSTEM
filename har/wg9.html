Shrimp and Vegetable Skewers

Duration: 20 minutes

Ingredients

Shrimp (150g, peeled and deveined)
Bell peppers (1 cup, diced)
Zucchini (1 medium, sliced)
Olive oil (1 tbsp)
mon juice (1 tbsp)


Directions:

Step 1: Thread shrimp, bell peppers, and zucchini onto skewers.
Step 2: Mix olive oil and lemon juice for a marinade.
Step 3: Brush the skewers with the marinade.
Step 4: Grill or bake until shrimp is cooked
Step 5: Serve with your favorite side
