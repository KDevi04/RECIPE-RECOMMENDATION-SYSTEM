tuna salad sandwich

Duration: 15 minutes

Ingredients

Canned Tuna (1 can, drained)
Whole Wheat Bread (2 slices)
Mayonnaise (2 tbsp)
tomamto (1)
Lettuce (1 cup)


Directions:

Step 1: In a bowl, mix drained tuna, mayonnaise, and chopped celery.
Step 2: Spread tuna mixture on whole wheat bread.
Step 3: Top with lettuce and the second slice of bread.
Step 4: Slice and serve
