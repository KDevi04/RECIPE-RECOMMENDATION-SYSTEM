Beef and Bean Burritos

Duration: 30 minutes

Ingredients

Ground Beef (200g)
Flour Tortillas (2)
Black Beans (1/2 cup, cooked)
Salsa (1/4 cup)
Sour Cream (2 tbsp)


Directions:

Step 1: Cook ground beef until browned.
Step 2: Warm tortillas and assemble with beef, black beans, salsa, and sour cream.
Step 3: Fold into burritos and serve
