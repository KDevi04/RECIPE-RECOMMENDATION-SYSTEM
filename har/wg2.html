Beef Stir Fry

Duration: 20 minutes

Ingredients

beef sirloin strip 250g
broccoli2cups
bell peppers 1 cup
soy sauce 3tbsp
olive oil 2tbsp


Directions:

Step 1: Stir-fry beef in olive oil until browned. Set aside.
Step 2: In the same pan, stir-fry broccoli and bell peppers until tender
Step 3: Add cooked beef back to the pan
Step 4: Pour soy sauce over the mixture and toss until well-coated
Step 5: Serve over rice or noodles.
