Grilled Chicken Breast

Duration: 30 minutes

Ingredients

chicken breast 300g
fettuccine pasta 200g
heavy cream 1 cup
parmesan cheese 1 cup
3 cloes garlic


Directions:

Step 1: Cook pasta according to package instructions.
Step 2: In a pan, sauté minced garlic in olive oil until fragrant.
Step 3: Add diced chicken breast and cook until browned.
Step 4: Pour in heavy cream, bring to a simmer, and stir in Parmesan cheese until melted.
Step 5: Combine cooked pasta with the creamy chicken sauce.
Step 6: Garnish with extra Parmesan and parsley.
