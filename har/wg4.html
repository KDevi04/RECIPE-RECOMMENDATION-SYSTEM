Pork Fried Rice

Duration: 35 minutes

Ingredients

Pork Tenderloin (200g)
White Rice (1 cup, cooked)
Frozen Mixed Vegetables (1 cup)
Soy Sauce (2 tbsp)
Eggs (2, beaten)


Directions:

Step 1: Cook rice according to package instructions.
Step 2: Sauté pork until fully cooked, add mixed vegetables.
Step 3: Push ingredients to the side, scramble eggs in the pan.
Step 4: Mix everything together and add soy sauce.
Step 5: Serve hot.
