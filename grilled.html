Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken

Duration: 40 minutes

Ingredients

6 boneless chicken breast halves
1tbs OF LEMON JUICE
1/4 CUP OLIVE OIL
6 CLOVES GARLIC
1tbs FRESH PARSLEY
1/4th tbs SALT & PEPPER


Directions:

Step 1: Place chicken in a resealable plastic bag set in a shallow bowl.
Step 2: For marinade, in a small bowl, combine oil, garlic, lemon peel, thyme, rosemary, crushed red pepper, salt and black pepper.
Step 3: Pour marinade over chicken. Seal bag; turn to coat chicken. Marinate in the refrigerator for 2 to 4 hours, turning bag occasionally.
Step 4: Drain chicken, discarding marinade. Place chicken on the rack of an uncovered grill directly over medium coals.
Step 5: Grill for 12 to 15 minutes or until chicken is no longer pink (165 degrees F), turning once halfway through grilling.
Step 6: If desired, garnish with fresh thyme sprigs and lemon wedges.
