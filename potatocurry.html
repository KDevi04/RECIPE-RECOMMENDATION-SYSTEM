One Pot Potato Curry

Duration: 40 minutes

Ingredients

POTATO 3-4
CHICKPEAS 1 BOWL
BROCOLLI 4-5 CLOVES
RED BELL PEPPERS 3-4
FRENCH SPINACH
ONION 2-3
Ginger 2 spoons
&
Garlic 5-6 cloves
TOMATO 2-DICED
COCONUT MILK 250ml
CUMIN 1 TABLESPOON
CORIANDER POWDER 1 TABLESPOON
GARAM MASALA 1 TABLESPOON
SALT AS PER TASTE


Directions:

Step 1: Heat vegetable oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
Step 2: Add chopped onions and sauté until they turn translucent.
Step 3: Add minced garlic and grated ginger. Sauté for another minute until fragrant.
Step 4: Add turmeric powder, coriander powder, and garam masala. Stir well to combine with the onions, garlic, and ginger.
Step 5: Add diced potatoes and chopped tomatoes to the pot. Mix everything together until the potatoes are well coated with the spices.
Step 6: Pour in the coconut milk and season with salt. Stir to combine.
Step 7: If you're using green chilies for added heat, add them to the pot at this point.
Step 8: Cover the pot and let the curry simmer over low heat for about 20-25 minutes, or until the potatoes are tender and cooked through. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.
Step 9: Once the potatoes are cooked, taste the curry and adjust the seasoning if needed.
Step 10: Garnish with fresh cilantro leaves before serving
