EASY PANEER TIKKA

Duration: 50 minutes

Ingredients

OLIVE OIL AS PER REQUIRED
GREEN CHILLI 1,CHOPPED
ONION 1/2 SLICED
GINGER 1/2 tsp & GARLIC 2 CLOVES
GARAM MASALA 1 tsp
TURMERIC POWDER 1/2 tsp
CUMIN POWDER 1/2 tsp
PANEER CUBES 200 gms
CHILLI POWDER 1 tsp
CURD 3/4 CUP
LEMON JUICE 2 tsp
SALT


Directions:

Step 1: Take curd in a bowl and whisk it well.
Step 2: Add chilli powder, cumin seed powder, ginger and garlic paste, chilli, fennel seeds powder, pepper powder, besan, 1 tsp oil, and salt, and mix well.
Step 3: Marinate the paneer pieces in the curd mixture for at least an hour.
Step 4: Brush a non-stick grill pan with oil and place the paneer in it. Cook on both sides, and baste with oil if needed, till it turns brown.
Step 5: Then add the dry lentils and water, and cook for about 18 minutes, until the lentils are cooked. If the lentils aren't cooked through and your liquid has evaporated, keep adding water until they are soft.
Step 6: Serve hot with sliced onions, lemon, and green chutney.
