Masoor Dal with Chicken

Duration: 45 minutes

Ingredients

chicken 100 grams
Onion 1
Tomato 1
Masoor dal 1/4
garlic 2 cloves


Directions:

Step 1: Heat oil in a pressure cooker or a pot over medium heat. Add chopped onions and sauté until they turn translucent. Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and slit green chili. Saute for another minute.
Step 2: Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they soften. Stir in turmeric powder, cumin powder, coriander powder, and red chili powder. Cook for a minute to toast the spices.
Step 3: Add rinsed masoor dal and chicken pieces to the pot. Mix well. Add enough water to cover the dal and chicken (about 1.5 to 2 cups).
Step 4: Season with salt to taste. If using a pressure cooker, close the lid and cook for about 2-3 whistles or until the dal and chicken are cooked through
Step 5: If using a pot, cover and cook until the dal and chicken are tender, stirring occasionally. Once cooked, garnish with fresh coriander leaves before serving.
