Fish Curry with Coconut Milk

Duration: 30 minutes

Ingredients

fish 200grams
coconut milk 1 tablespoon
Onion 1
Tomato 1
Coriander leaves (optional)


Directions:

Step 1: Rinse the fish fillets under cold water and pat them dry with paper towels. Cut the fillets into bite-sized pieces and set aside.
Step 2: Heat coconut oil in a pan over medium heat. Add chopped onions and sauté until they turn translucent, about 3-4 minutes. Stir in minced garlic, grated ginger, and chopped green chili. Cook for another 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
Step 3: Add chopped tomatoes to the pan and cook until they soften and break down, about 5-6 minutes. Stir in ground turmeric, ground coriander, ground cumin, and chili powder. Cook for a minute until the spices are aromatic.
Step 4: Pour in the coconut milk and bring the curry to a gentle simmer. Let the curry simmer for about 5 minutes to allow the flavors to meld together.
Step 5: Carefully add the fish pieces to the simmering curry. Gently stir to coat the fish with the sauce.
Step 6: Cover the pan and let the fish cook in the curry for about 8-10 minutes or until it is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.
