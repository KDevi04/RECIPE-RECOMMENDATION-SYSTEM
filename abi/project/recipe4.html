Zucchini Noodles with Pesto and Grilled Chicken

Duration: 25-35 minutes

Ingredients

chicken 150 grams
olive oil 1 tablespoon
basil leaves 1 cup
medium-sized zucchini, spiralized into noodles
parmesan cheese 1/3 cup


Directions:

Step 1: Season the chicken breast with salt, pepper, and your choice of herbs and spices. Grill the chicken over medium-high heat for 4-6 minutes per side or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
Step 2: Allow the chicken to rest for a few minutes before slicing it into strips. Spiralize the zucchini into noodles using a spiralizer.
Step 3: Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Saute the zucchini noodles for 2-3 minutes until just tender. Season with salt and pepper.
Step 4: If using store-bought pesto, skip to the next step.
Step 5: If making homemade pesto, blend basil, Parmesan cheese, pine nuts, garlic, and olive oil in a food processor until smooth. Season with salt, pepper, and lemon juice to taste.
Step 6: Top the zucchini noodles with grilled chicken strips. Optionally, garnish with grated Parmesan cheese, pine nuts, and fresh basil leaves.
