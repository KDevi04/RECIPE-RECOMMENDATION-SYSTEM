Egg Curry

Duration: 30 minutes

Ingredients

Eggs 2
Onion 1
Tomato 1
Garam Masala 1/4
garlic 1/2 inch


Directions:

Step 1: Hard-boil the eggs, peel them, and cut each egg into halves. Set aside. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
Step 2: Add chopped onions and sauté until they turn golden brown. Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and green chili. Saute for another minute.
Step 3: Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they turn soft and mushy. Stir in turmeric powder, coriander powder, red chili powder, and salt. Cook the spices for a couple of minutes.
Step 4: Add water to achieve the desired consistency of the curry. Bring it to a gentle simmer. Gently place the halved boiled eggs into the curry. Cover and simmer for 5-7 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld.
Step 5: Sprinkle garam masala powder and garnish with fresh cilantro leaves. Serve hot with rice or bread of your choice.
