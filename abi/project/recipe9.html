Egg Bhurji

Duration: 15 minutes

Ingredients

Eggs 2
Onion 1
Tomato 1
ghee 1 teaspoon
garlic 1/2 inch


Directions:

Step 1: Heat oil or ghee in a pan over medium heat. Add chopped onions and green chilies to the pan. Sauté until the onions turn translucent.
Step 2: Add chopped tomatoes to the pan and cook until they soften. Beat the eggs in a bowl and pour them into the pan.
Step 3: Add turmeric powder, red chili powder, garam masala powder, and salt to the eggs.
Step 4: Stir the mixture continuously until the eggs are cooked and well combined with the onion-tomato mixture.
Step 5: Garnish with fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot with roti, bread, or rice.
