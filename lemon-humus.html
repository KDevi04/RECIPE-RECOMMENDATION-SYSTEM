LEMON-ROASTED VEGETABLE HUMMUS BOWLS

Duration: 15 minutes

Ingredients

OLIVE OIL AS PER REQUIRED
BROCOLLI 1 1/2CUPS
CAULIFLOWER 1 1/2 FLORETS
GINGER 1/2 tsp & GARLIC 2 CLOVES
OREGANO
RED-BELL PEPPER 3/4 CUP
ZUCCHINI 3/4 CUP
TRICOLOR QUINOA 2 CUPS
HUMMUS 1 CUP
LEMON JUICE 2 tsp
SALT
AVOCADO 1 MEDIUM


Directions:

Step 1: Preheat oven to 425 degrees F. Combine cauliflower, broccoli and garlic on a rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with oregano and salt; stir to coat. Roast for 10 minutes.
Step 2: Add bell pepper and zucchini to the vegetables in the pan; stir to combine. Roast until the vegetables are crisp-tender and lightly browned, 10 to 15 minutes more. Sprinkle lemon zest over the vegetables; set aside to cool before assembling bowls.
Step 3: Divide the roasted vegetables among 4 single-serving containers. Top each with 1/2 cup quinoa and 1/4 cup hummus and add a lemon wedge to each container. Seal the containers and refrigerate for up to 4 days. To serve, squeeze the lemon wedge over the bowl and top with one-fourth avocado, diced.
